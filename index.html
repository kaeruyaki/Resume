As a creative supervisor who has worked in animation and advertising since 2008, I’ve spent my career crafting Academy Award-winning hybrid animation projects that have spanned traditional hand-drawn, 3D computer graphics, stop-motion, and live-action puppetry.

Because I am a seasoned supervisor, I have fluency in the logistics of animation production, including planning workflows, bidding sequences, scheduling, casting shots, and managing creative teams. I am able to discern the signal from the noise, and make production-friendly creative decisions with practical methodologies in mind. Because of my deep understanding of animation software and techniques, directing through demonstration is second nature to me.

An artist at heart, I was trained in traditional fine arts skills. My eye for color, composition, and aesthetic tone helps me steer the visual style to help drive the narrative. I rely on my team members and their individual expertise to get the best possible creative result.

Having spent years as a professor for animation courses, teaching comes naturally to me. I rely on this experience to ensure my teams are clear on creative prompts and methodologies. With my expertise in communication, I’m able to explain processes at varying levels of complexity, and to listen carefully to concerns or needs from team members, while giving clear and honest feedback about what is possible. I firmly believe that encouragement and a sense of ownership are the greatest tools to inspire a team to deliver their best possible work, and I work hard to ensure every member feels welcomed and included in the process.

Interested in my next creative management role: Visual Effects Supervisor, Stylization Supervisor, or Creative Director.
